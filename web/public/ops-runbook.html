Operations investigation

The dashboard reports stored observations. A recent beat does not prove delivery, restored backups, or a successful learning job. Unknown means there is no current evidence.

Async workers

Compare the beat and last successful cycle with the observation time. Check API service status and its recent logs through your approved server access. Confirm the database is available, then inspect the durable queue for failed or unfinished work. Mail can run successfully while delivery is disabled.

Assessment, desk retention, calendar and game workers now record each completed cycle. Failed cycles preserve the prior success. A missing receipt still requires investigation; it is not proof that a task has stopped. A legacy assessment observation appears only until its first standard cycle receipt. Investigate before restarting; preserve durable jobs and existing receipts.

Private operator reference: Oracle Paper Workbench Runbook, “V.12 operations dashboard”.

Systemd timers

Outcome collectors run after the actual bank publication, paired backup and stats jobs. A newly installed collector remains unknown until its first actual job run. Use systemctl list-timers --all and the named unit’s status and journal through approved server access. Check the last actual execution result and its output receipt. An active timer alone does not prove a successful job.

Bank publication uses the existing authority and reviewed pipeline. Do not bypass publication guards, replay a frozen release, or repair bank records with SQL. Private reference: Oracle runbook, current operations and recovery sections.

Service probes

An unavailable API or database appears as a failed snapshot, never zero counts. Pool usage is for the process handling the request and includes that request’s connection. Check waiting requests, server logs and database availability before changing pool limits.

The host collector samples disk headroom and verifies the newest completed paired backup every five minutes. Free space below 10 GiB needs attention; stale collection after 15 minutes is visible. Backup success uses its actual creation time, becomes stale after 48 hours, and never falls back silently over a newer incomplete or corrupt snapshot. Archive and database digests plus the dump index are checked, with a private cache invalidated on file identity or metadata changes. A verified backup is separate from a successful restore rehearsal. Checkout remains separately gated; a disabled webhook metric is not zero traffic.

Economy watch

Totals use the completed IST day. Minting sums positive ledger amounts including adjustments; spending is shown separately from recorded redemptions. A cap count means an account reached its recorded daily XP or point limit, not the number of rejected earning attempts.

Seven-day cap streaks and new-account earnings are review cues. Inspect existing account and reward receipts in Rewards & accounts before deciding whether anything is wrong. Do not infer abuse, grant points or change accounts from these flags alone. Lists show at most 50 accounts with an explicit overflow indicator.

Moderation pulse

Question, discussion, material, creator and private-desk report queues are counted separately. A blank median means no open reports. Counts older than 48 hours support the moderation target; they do not show how quickly resolved reports were handled. Open the existing scoped moderation tools and retain resolution evidence.

Bounded retention

The hourly account worker removes old unusable web/mobile credentials in batches of 500 and retains their actual creation timestamp for account support. It never turns cleanup time into learner activity. Current credentials remain. Old combined-filter request keys expire only after 30 completed IST dates and 30 days without changes; durable attempt, reward, billing and adoption receipts stay.

Guest removal requires generated guest identity, no registered/closed account, credentials expired for over 12 months, and no linked data. Current and future foreign keys, alternate/composite keys and legacy text ID references protect data. Eligible guests are filtered before a 20-account batch is selected. A rolled-back sweep never claims success. Counts appear in the worker receipt.

Site notice

A global owner uses Operations to publish plain text, optionally with an expiry. Existing staff MFA and same-origin controls apply. Saves are versioned and recorded in the owner’s account event history. Reload after a conflict. Clear notice removes the announcement while retaining audit history. Public pages refresh it each minute and hide it at expiry. Study and account actions continue normally.

Monthly paired restore and checklist

The dashboard’s restore age requires an actual isolated restore of the newest paired archive and database; checksum/index checks alone do not qualify. Through approved server access, run sudo /srv/api/.venv/bin/python /srv/api/restore-rehearsal.py --run. It verifies source digests, restores private files with readback hashes, restores a disposable PostgreSQL database, compares the restored bank to its workflow checksum under a read-only application role, then removes temporary resources. A verified receipt is written only after cleanup. An unsuccessful attempt preserves the prior success date and needs investigation. At 40 days, a successful rehearsal is overdue.

This tests the contents and date of that backup. Original source assets and offsite recovery remain separate responsibilities in the private Oracle runbook. It never promotes a restored bank, switches the live database or runs a publisher.

  1. Run and receipt the actual paired restore.
  2. Review dependency and CVE reports; do not label unchecked packages clean.
  3. Check TLS certificates and both study and family sites.
  4. Review available slow-query logs and configuration.
  5. Check economy diagnostics and moderation backlog.
  6. Inspect disk headroom and log rotation.
  7. Check every worker and collector; missing/stale/failed remains visible.

Record the human checklist outcome in DEVLOG with the dated private evidence. A fresh rehearsal is not a claim of seven uninterrupted healthy days.
