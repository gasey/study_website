# Study each exam by subject and topic

Deployed 28 October 2026. Every public exam card on `/exams` now links to its `Subjects & topics` view. The exam page also has a hero action and tab for that view. Learners can search its subjects/topics, expand a subject, and browse or practise at subject, topic or subtopic level. The exam question browser has matching filters that can be combined with paper, section, answer status, answer pool and personal status. Subject/topic/subtopic filters are retained in the saved exam checkpoint.

The `/api/collections/{id}/study-topics` endpoint counts distinct canonical questions in the exam's browsable listed papers, using only approved taxonomy paths. Counts are calculated separately at each level, so a question with multiple approved paths can occur in multiple subject/topic counts without inflating the exam total. Untagged questions stay available through all-question and paper browse. The provision uses the actual paper question pool; it does not claim to be a complete official syllabus or invent missing tags. Group B gazetted currently has subject coverage but no approved topic tags, so its subject cards remain usable without narrower topics.
