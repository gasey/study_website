# Paper identity and learning classification

Use [QUESTION-TAGGING-RULES.md](QUESTION-TAGGING-RULES.md) for every current
classification decision. It is the single tagging policy, including GK/GS,
Current Affairs, English, region/language, secondary tags and ambiguity.

Use one shared bank and preserve each exam's source appearances. Printed paper
headings and verified schemes determine exam labels; tested content determines
learning paths. Cross-exam GK/GS browsing does not rename a UDC paper General Studies.

- [Learning tags](https://study.hawayu.in/admin/bank?tab=retag): inspect current
  primary/related paths, preview checked corrections and submit through the normal
  review/publication workflow. Applied receipt and readback confirm completion.
- [Modules and schemes](https://study.hawayu.in/admin/bank?tab=organization):
  configure exact applicable editions and paper mappings after source inspection.
- [Paper readiness](https://study.hawayu.in/admin/bank?tab=organization&organization_view=paper):
  inspect original layout and section membership.

Owner/admin decisions use their current permissions and actual attribution.
Ordinary community submissions retain their independent-review requirements.
Follow [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md) and the [publication runbook](ORACLE-PAPER-WORKBENCH-RUNBOOK.md).
The [original release history](history/tagging-before-20261107/classification-and-paper-identity.md)
is archived; old pending statuses, local-write locations and blanket review rules
are not current operating instructions.
