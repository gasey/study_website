Scoring

How points work

There are two numbers. Score measures how much you practised and played this month, and it drives the leaderboard. Reputation measures how much the community trusts your contributions, and it unlocks tiers. Your own progress is tracked either way.

Choose how competitive you want it

Score
Monthly, resets on the 1st
Score is the XP you earn in a calendar month. It drives the monthly board; the all-time board adds every month together.
Reputation
Lifetime, never resets
Earned when others confirm your work. Contributor at 200, Mentor at 1,000, Scholar at 2,500. Contributions also add to Score.
Progress
Private, always on
Answered, accuracy by subject, mistakes cleared, mocks finished. Visible only to you and the desk, if you share it.
