Before enabling original simulation:

1. Verify exact-paper rules and final-key provenance, all printed positions and ordered choices. Resolve outstanding source defects without fabricating missing content. Keep the discovered 2025 source-page/text findings separate from explanation-only publications.
2. Establish the exact compensation policy from authority evidence. The 24 compensation appearances remain held and excluded from ordinary scoring. A full original paper cannot silently omit them or pretend they have an option answer.
3. Introduce an explicit, versioned original-paper mode with frozen printed positions, source snapshots, final key and per-item outcomes. Keep existing practice sessions/history intact. Preserve source explanations separately from key provenance.
4. Use exact rational score units for +2 and -2/3 (for example, thirds of a mark); round only for display. Handle correct, wrong, blank and authority-compensated outcomes independently. Confirm whether compensation depends on attempting an item; do not guess.
5. Test compensation, unanswered/held items, option ordering, timer/resume, subsequent key corrections and history preservation before activation. Do not infer overall qualifying/cutoff rules from the cover's per-question marks.

Explanations are a separate teaching backlog: an official key does not verify its explanation. This pass prioritizes checked explanations and dated revision guidance; it does not certify source completeness or original simulation readiness. 2012/2013 recovery remains deferred.
